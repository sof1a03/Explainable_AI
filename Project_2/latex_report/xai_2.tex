\subsection{Experimentation Setup} \label{subsec:Experimentation}

To evaluate the algorithm presented in Section \ref{sec:algorithm}, a user survey was conducted using FeedbackFruits\footnote{https://feedbackfruits.com}. The survey was generated by the professor and the participants were randomly allocated students from the Explainable AI course at Utrecht University.

Participants were asked to evaluate $5$ different natural language explanations that were generated using the previously described algorithm and a predefined input. For each explanation, participants were asked to evaluate 6 questions (seen in Table \ref{tab:questions}) on a 5-point Likert scale, ranging from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree). The users were provided both a description of a scenario (following a pre-defined template) and the corresponding explanations.

Each of the questions provided is used to evaluate different criteria for human explanations:

Overall, this shows that the provided natural language explanations were satisfactory to the users, but there are some areas in which they can be improved.


Through the mean, mode, and median values one can see that the areas with the most need for improvement are those tested through Q1, Q6, and Q3, more specifically the following criteria: Transparency, Satisfaction, and Sufficiency.
